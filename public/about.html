About Party Games

Last updated: November 2026

What this is

Party Games is a free way to play group games when everyone is in the same room. One big screen (a TV or laptop) shows the shared game, and everybody uses their own phone as a controller: tap your answers, see your own cards, and watch the scores on the big screen. There are no accounts and nothing to install.

The platform is built so that other developers can contribute their own games.

Privacy: the short version

  • No accounts, passwords or email addresses.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Your nickname and your game results exist only while a room exists, and are deleted afterwards.

Privacy: the details

What you give us. When you join a game you choose a nickname. Your nickname, your answers and guesses, and your scores are kept in the server's memory only while the room exists. Rooms are deleted after about 6 hours without activity, and everything is lost if the server restarts. Nothing is saved to a database, and none of it is sold or shared.

What other people can see. Everyone in a room, and anyone watching the big screen, sees your nickname and your game actions. Please don't use your full name or any personal information as a nickname.

What stays on your device. So that you can rejoin if you close the page by accident, your browser remembers your seat (the game, the room code, your nickname and a secret seat code) for up to 12 hours. This is stored only on your device and is never used to track you. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting and server logs. The site is hosted by Render (render.com) in Frankfurt, Germany. Like almost any web server, the hosting service handles your IP address and technical details of each request (such as the page requested and your browser type) in order to deliver the site and keep it secure, and it may keep them in short-lived logs. Under the GDPR an IP address counts as personal data. These logs are not used to identify or track you.

Outside services used by some games. Some games load material from other services. When they do, that service can see the IP address of the device or server that asked for it:

  • Trivia questions come from the Open Trivia Database. Our server fetches them, so only the server's address is seen.
  • Name That Tune plays short audio previews from Deezer in the big screen's browser, so Deezer sees that device's address.
  • Where Am I uses street-level photos from Panoramax and maps from OpenStreetMap. List only the services you actually use.

Your rights. You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. Because nothing is linked to your identity and nothing is kept beyond a game, there is usually nothing to find, but you are welcome to ask by email. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
